Who are the Party Rats?

Party Rats is a collective of curious, creative and slightly mischievous people who like making memorable things together.

Every project starts differently. We gather the right mix of designers, makers, cooks, musicians and problem-solvers around the idea, then obsess over the details until it becomes something we would genuinely want to attend.

We choose the projects that make us smile. The unexpected ones. The ones that make people say, “I have absolutely no idea how they pulled this off.”
